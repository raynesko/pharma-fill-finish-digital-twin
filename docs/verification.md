# Verification

Sub-epic SE4. Verification asks whether the code does what the model says it does, and validation asks whether the model is close enough to a real line. This page covers verification only. Validation (face validation with a practitioner, T4.2.2) is separate. The line is SIMULATED and BENCHMARK-PARAMETERIZED. NOT PLANT DATA.

How to reproduce:
- `python tasks.py test` runs the fast suite, including the invariants.
- `python tasks.py verify` runs the slow analytical checks and regenerates the table in section 2.

## 1. Invariants (T4.1.1, `tests/verification/test_invariants.py`)
| Invariant | How it is tested |
|---|---|
| Vial conservation: started = packed + scrapped + WIP at every instant | 20 random configurations, sampled off the event grid every 7.31 min. They vary station rates, failures, minor stops, sub-lot size (10/50/100), buffer size, staffing, changeover scale, 1-2 AVI units and a 1-4 batch mix |
| State time adds up: each station unit's state minutes = horizon, with no overlapping intervals | Same 20 configurations, both before and after the calendar overlay |
| No-failure throughput = bottleneck rate | 5 random rate sets, no failures, changeovers or quality losses, one 200,000-vial batch. Steady packing rate is within 0.5% of the slowest rate |
| Edge cases | An empty schedule is rejected by validation. Single batches of 1, 99, 100 and 150 vials complete, need no changeover and are QA-released |
| Determinism and common random numbers | Same seed gives an identical state log. Changing one station, or adding a parallel unit, leaves every other station's draws unchanged (`tests/unit/test_runner.py`) |
| Equipment OEE does not depend on QA (D5) | Identical state logs with QA off, on, or on with a 14-day hold (`tests/unit/test_qa_release.py`) |

Line coverage on 2026-11-17: `ffdt.sim` and `ffdt.metrics` at 99% (target 90%). The fast suite runs 160 tests in about 53 s (target 60 s); 3 slow checks run via `python tasks.py verify` and in CI.

## 2. Analytical cross-checks (T4.1.2, `tests/verification/test_analytic.py`)
The real `Machine` and `MonitoredStore` classes are configured as textbook queues (`ffdt.experiments.analytic`):
- **Service time** = sub-lot vials / rate. Sizes are geometric with mean 1,000 at 1,000 vpm, so service is exponential with mean 1 min. Its second moment is within 0.05% of exponential.
- **Wq theory** uses Pollaczek-Khinchine with the exact moments, which reduces to λ/(μ(μ−λ)) for M/M/1.
- **M/M/1/K:** arrivals that find K in the system (K−1 waiting plus 1 in service) are lost. P_block = (1−ρ)ρ^K / (1−ρ^(K+1)).
- **Availability:** a never-starved machine with operation-dependent exponential failures and lognormal repairs (CV 0.8). A = MTBF/(MTBF+MTTR) holds for any repair distribution.
- **Confidence intervals** are t-based 95% over 30 replications, 20,000 customers each.
- **Little's Law** is an identity for a buffer that starts and ends empty, as every buffer in a completed terminating run does. Agreement to machine precision therefore checks the queue *bookkeeping*: no lost, duplicated or mistimed entries. It is not evidence about model behavior.
- **Two estimates sit just below theory** (Wq −0.7%, P_block −1.2%), but both theory values are inside the CIs. The M/M/1/K one is a near-miss: theory sits close to the top of its CI. A run with more replications would show whether the gap is real.

## 3. Run length, warm-up and replications (T4.1.3, `notebooks/04_warmup_reps.ipynb`)
**Mode: terminating.** A campaign starts on an empty, cleaned and cleared line and ends when the last batch is packed, just as the real line does. The start-up transient is part of the system, so no warm-up is deleted in baseline or scenario runs.

**Welch check (verification aid only).** The steady-state model is one 5,000,000-vial batch with no changeovers inside the horizon, an always-on calendar, and failures and quality on. 10 replications × 168 h, moving-average window 6 h. The moving average first reaches within 5% of its second-half mean at **hour 3**. Steady output is about 12,800 vials/h (213 vpm, 89% of the filler's 240 vpm nominal), the remainder being breakdowns, minor stops and AVI rejects. RESULT, draft config.

**Replications.** A pilot of 20 terminating campaigns gives these t-based requirements for a 95% CI half-width ≤ 2% of the mean:

| Metric | Pilot mean | CV | n required |
|---|---|---|---|
| good vials per PPT hour (primary) | 8,256 | 3.5% | 15 |
| line OEE (primary) | 0.573 | 3.5% | 15 |
| good vials per calendar hour | 3,686 | 16% | 254 |
| makespan (h) | 165 | 11% | 129 |
| mean batch lead time (h) | 84 | 31% | 907 |

**Chosen: n_reps = 30** (`configs/experiment.yaml`, seed 20261116). That is double the primary-metric requirement, because the pilot standard deviation is itself an estimate. Calendar-hour throughput, makespan and lead time are reported with their wider CIs and are not used for pre-registered decisions. Their variance comes from the weekend spill (planning/08) and the QA review time CV.
